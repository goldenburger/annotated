Get the annotated extension

annotated is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. You can install it directly in Chrome in about a minute.

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode at the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped annotated-extension folder.
  4. Pin annotated from the puzzle-piece menu, open any article, YouTube video, podcast or post on X, and click the annotated button.

Sign in with Google from the panel to publish annotations everyone can see.
